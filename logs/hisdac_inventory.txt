===== /pl/active/Leyk_Lab/data/HISDAC_US_V2/BUI_README.txt
These files are part of the dataset "Historical built-up intensity layer series for the U.S. 1810 – 2015,"
contained in the dataverse “HISDAC-US: Historical Settlement Data Compilation for the United States” 
(https://dataverse.harvard.edu/dataverse/hisdacus).

Reference:
Ahn, Yoonjung;Leyk, Stefan;Uhl, Johannes H.; McShane, Caitlin M., 2023, "Gridded Settlement Data for the Conterminous United States Over 200 years",
https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/1WB9E4, Harvard Dataverse

Related Publication:
Leyk, Stefan; Uhl, Johannes H., 2018, "HISDAC-US: Historical Settlement Data Compilation for the Conterminous United States Over 200 Years," Scientific Data 5:180175
https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2018.175
Uhl, J. H. et al. Fine-grained, spatio-temporal datasets measuring 200 years of land development in the United States. Earth Syst. Sci. Data 96Ð114 (2020). doi:10.4324/9781315019444-13

This dataset contains the following files:

- BUI.tar.gz: Archive contains the time series of BUI raster layers between 1810 and 2015 (BUI_1810 … BUI_2015) with semi-decadal temporal resolution.

Abbreviations and explanations:
BUI:		Built-up intensity. Integer-valued raster datasets containing indoor building gross area sum per pixel area in square feet.

Data source: 
- Zillow Transaction and Assessment Dataset (ZTRAX) (c) Zillow Inc.
- Parcel (c) Parcel Atlas
- BuildZero Open City Model
Spatial resolution: 250m

These files are part of the dataset
"Historical built-up property records (BUPR) - gridded surfaces for the U.S. from 1810 to 2020",
contained in the dataverse “HISDAC-US: Historical Settlement Data Compilation for the United States” 
(https://dataverse.harvard.edu/dataverse/hisdacus).

Reference:
Ahn, Y., Leyk, S., Uhl, J. H., & McShane, C. M. (2024). An Integrated Multi-Source Dataset for Measuring Settlement Evolution in the United States from 1810 to 2020. Scientific Data 2024 11:1, 11(1), 1Ð16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-024-03081-x
https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/45B8IU, Harvard Dataverse. 

Related Publication:
Leyk, Stefan; Uhl, Johannes H., 2018, "HISDAC-US: Historical Settlement Data Compilation for the Conterminous United States Over 200 Years," Scientific Data 5:180175
https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2018.175
Uhl, J. H. et al. Fine-grained, spatio-temporal datasets measuring 200 years of landdevelopment in the United States. Earth Syst. Sci. Data 96Ð114 (2020). doi:10.4324/9781315019444-13


The gridded surfaces (*.tif) contained in the BUPR.tar.gz archive represent built-up property records (i.e., buildings or building units)
contained within grid cells of 250x250m, for each half-decade from 1810 to 2020.
Moreover, there is a file called BUPR_contemp.tif which contains all built-up property records from the underlying ZTRAX dataset,
regardless the value or availability of the built year attribute. Thus, BUPR_contemp.tif represents the spatial distribution of built-up 
property records approximately in 2016, when the underlying ZTRAX data were compiled.

Abbreviations and explanations:
BUPR:			Built-up property records. Individual built-up property records in ZTRAX, Parcel, and BuildZero Open City Model representing a building or building unit, depending on ownership situation.

Data source: 
- Zillow Transaction and Assessment Dataset (ZTRAX) (c) Zillow Inc.
- Parcel (c) Parcel Atlas
- BuildZero Open City Model

Spatial resolution: 250m
Coordinate reference system:
USA Contiguous Albers Equal Area Conic USGS version (EPSG:5070)
https://spatialreference.org/ref/sr-org/usa_contiguous_albers_equal_area_conic_usgs_version-2/
